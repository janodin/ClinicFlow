Setup Instructions

Follow these steps to activate Messenger booking via n8n.

  1. 1 Go to your Meta Developer Dashboard and get your App ID, App Secret, Page ID, and Page Access Token.
  2. 2 Enter the Meta app and Page credentials, then click Save Settings.
  3. 3 In your Meta Developer Dashboard, go to your App -> Messenger -> Webhooks. Set the callback URL to the Meta Callback URL below and subscribe to messages and messaging_postbacks.
  4. 4 Patients can now message your Facebook Page to book appointments. The bot will guide them through service selection, date, time, and confirmation.
