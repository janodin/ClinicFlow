Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

ClinicFlow ("we," "our," or "us") operates the ClinicFlow appointment booking platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service to book appointments at healthcare clinics.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: When you book an appointment, we collect your full name, phone number, and email address. You may also provide a reason for your visit. This information is shared only with the clinic you booked with.

Account and Clinic Workspace Information: When clinic owners or staff create and manage an account, we collect account identity information, consent records, and clinic workspace details such as clinic name, timezone, contact details, services, prices, booking settings, and business hours.

Facebook Messenger Data: If you interact with our Messenger bot, we may collect your Facebook PSID (page-scoped ID) and the content of your messages for the purpose of facilitating appointment bookings and clinic communications.

Appointment Data: We store appointment details including service type, date/time, status, and reference codes for the purpose of managing your bookings.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your appointments at your chosen clinic
  • To create, configure, and administer clinic workspaces for authorized clinic owners and staff
  • To send you appointment confirmations and reminders via Facebook Messenger
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To improve our platform and service functionality

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Clinic Sharing: Your personal information (name, phone, email, reason for visit) is shared with the specific clinic where you book an appointment, solely for the purpose of providing healthcare services.

Facebook: Message content and your PSID are processed by Meta's Messenger platform. We encourage you to review Meta's Privacy Policy.

Service Providers: We may share information with vendors who assist in operating our platform, including hosting and analytics, under confidentiality obligations.

Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Appointment records are retained in accordance with the clinic's data retention policies. You may request deletion of your data by contacting the clinic directly or using our contact information below.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes SSL encryption, secure servers, and access controls.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, please contact the clinic where you booked your appointment, as they are the data controller for your patient records.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Our platform may use cookies to maintain session state and improve user experience. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
